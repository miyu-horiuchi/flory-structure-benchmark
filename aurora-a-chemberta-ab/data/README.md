# Aurora A inputs and provenance

- `Lake2018_panel.csv`: This 24-compound table transcribes the reported α mean, SEM and n from Table S2 and the no-TPX2 Kd (nM), SEM and n from SI Fig. S9B/C of Lake et al., *Quantitative conformational profiling of kinase inhibitors reveals origins of selectivity for Aurora kinase activation states*, PNAS (2018), [DOI 10.1073/pnas.1811158115](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1811158115). With-TPX2 Kd was not tabulated and was not imputed. SNS-314’s α is poorly constrained (SI Fig. S10). The compound assignments were checked against the panel in Fig. 2A and structures in SI Fig. S2. Isomeric structures were sourced from [PubChem](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/) and cross-checked by full InChIKey; [ChEMBL CHEMBL3600873](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/chembl/compound_report_card/CHEMBL3600873/) supplied the stereospecific MK-5108 record. RDKit 2026.9.2 produced the canonical isomeric SMILES.
- `chembl_aurka_exact_match_activities.csv` and `chembl_aurka_endpoint_coverage.csv`: Full-InChIKey matching and coverage summaries for [ChEMBL CHEMBL4722](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/chembl/target_report_card/CHEMBL4722/) (human Aurora A), including K4DD. Only positive finite values with standard relation `=` and known units count toward the coverage summaries. No connectivity-only match was promoted. ChEMBL data are attributed to ChEMBL/EMBL-EBI and available under [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/).
- `chembl_assay_CHEMBL3991630.json` and `chembl_document_CHEMBL3991601.json`: Direct ChEMBL API records for [the assay](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/chembl/api/data/assay/CHEMBL3991630.json) and [the document](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/chembl/api/data/document/CHEMBL3991601.json). The primary is the largest single comparable Kd assay (15/24); no values from other assays are pooled. Klaeger et al., *The target landscape of clinical kinase drugs*, Science (2017), [DOI 10.1126/science.aan4368](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aan4368), measured binding in human cell lysates with Kinobeads and mass-spectrometry readout. ChEMBL lists no variant sequence. The reported label is `9 - log10(Kd_nM)`.

ChEMBL had numeric Kd coverage for 19/24 across assays, and K4DD had 9/24 across assay conditions. No comparable independent assay covers all 24. Lake SI Fig. S11 plots an independent inhibition assay but does not tabulate per-compound values; Fig. S9 no-TPX2 Kd covers all 24 but shares the cooperativity fit, so it is sensitivity only. [PRIDE PXD062561](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/pride/archive/projects/PXD062561) measured HDX of N-Myc with and without Aurora A, not of this inhibitor panel (0/24).
