# Exploratory primary grouped-scaffold sensitivity — 2026-11-23 UTC

Added after seeing the sealed primary results and before training any grouped folds. This post-hoc comparison is not part of the sealed prespec, cannot change its conclusion, and does not replace the sealed leave-one-typed-Bemis–Murcko-scaffold-out result. The independent ChEMBL primary panel and pKd labels are unchanged. Lake-coupled sensitivity continues on the sealed split; its 24 compounds cannot fit into three or four groups of four to five compounds each.

Use three folds of five compounds. Start from the sealed typed scaffold assignments and retain all eligible members of each scaffold group together. Sort nonempty scaffold groups by decreasing size and then by canonical typed scaffold SMILES. Assign each whole group to the fold with the fewest compounds so far, breaking ties by fold ID (`G01`, `G02`, `G03`). The grouping looks at compound counts and scaffold identities only, not label or feature values. Train on each fold's complement and record all memberships.

Apart from splitting, keep the sealed primary recipe: Model A and B, LoRA and frozen, five seeds, fold-mean and cooperativity-only controls, the same ten shuffled and ten Gaussian blocks, identical fold-local scaling, training, aggregation and metrics, and the paired compound bootstrap. The reason for measuring this sensitivity is that a singleton test fold has no within-fold ranking; the sealed primary had 13 singleton folds of 14, and its fold-mean control scored ρ −0.9991. The protocol owner's ERK2 fold-mean control scored −0.82 with a spurious +1.03 Δρ.
